Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 August 2026 · MVP edition

Plain-language summary: Orbit stores the account, conversations, files, agent configuration, and connections needed to provide the service. Connected services are accessed only after explicit authorization, and secrets are encrypted at rest.

Data we process

  • Account details: email, display name, sessions and security events.
  • Workspace content: prompts, agent messages, memory notes, uploaded materials and generated artifacts.
  • Connection data: OAuth grants, provider configuration and synchronized content selected by the user.
  • Operational data: request identifiers, errors, performance and security logs.

How data is used

To operate Orbit, orchestrate agents, synchronize authorized sources, secure accounts, diagnose failures and improve reliability. Orbit does not sell personal data.

AI providers and connected services

Content is sent to a selected AI provider or connector only when required by the requested operation. Each provider processes data under its own terms. Access can be revoked from Orbit and, where supported, from the provider account.

Retention and deletion

Workspace data is retained until deleted by the user or account owner. Operational backups are retained for a limited recovery window. Account deletion removes active account data; backup copies expire through normal retention.

Security

Production traffic uses TLS, session cookies are HttpOnly and Secure, integration secrets are encrypted, and database access is restricted to the application network. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may access, export, correct or delete your content through Orbit. You may revoke a connector at any time. For privacy requests, contact the operator address published with the production domain.

Changes

The production operator must add its legal name, jurisdiction, contact address and any region-specific disclosures before public commercial launch.
